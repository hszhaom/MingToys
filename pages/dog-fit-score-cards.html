What a grooming label means in practice

A Golden Retriever has a medium grooming label and a high shedding label. The first describes time spent on the dog’s coat. The second helps you plan for loose hair at home. The Golden Retriever Club of America describes weekly brushing for much of the year and daily brushing during heavy sheds. Could someone take over the extra brushing on the weeks you travel? Read the club’s coat-care guidance.

Low, medium and high are broad categories. They do not predict an individual dog’s health, a child’s safety or a grooming bill. For a budget, ask a local groomer which services a quote includes, then compare it with the allowance in the Dog Cost Calculator.
