This issue tracker is public, so please do not post private account details, personal addresses, payment information, or veterinary records.

What We Review

PetStorie reviews correction requests, broken links, privacy questions, advertising-policy concerns, unclear breed guidance, and requests to clarify source or editorial language. We cannot diagnose a pet, recommend medication, or provide emergency veterinary advice through the website.

What to Include

  • The page URL involved.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Any correction, clarification, or policy concern you want reviewed.

Private Information

Because the current contact channel is public, keep reports focused on the page and the issue. Do not include addresses, phone numbers, payment records, private medical records, or identifying details about another person. If a future private email channel is added, this page will be updated.
