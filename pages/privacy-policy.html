Privacy notice

Privacy Policy

This site provides informational content and uses limited third-party services for analytics and advertising. By using the site, you understand that some technical data may be processed to operate the website, measure traffic, and deliver ads.

Site Operator

PetStorie is an independent personal website. Privacy questions can be sent to zhaoming990@gmail.com.

Accounts, Comments, Email Lists, and Affiliate Links

PetStorie does not provide user accounts, login features, public comments, newsletter signup forms, paid memberships, or checkout features. The site does not currently use affiliate marketing links. If those features are added later, this policy will be updated before they are used.

Analytics

PetStorie uses GoatCounter to measure page traffic. Like most analytics tools, it may process technical data such as page URLs, referrers, and basic browser information in order to generate aggregate visitor statistics.

PetStorie does not currently use a user account analytics system and does not ask readers to identify themselves for normal browsing.

Advertising

PetStorie is configured to use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on visits to this site and other websites. Readers can review how Google uses information from sites and apps at Google's partner sites policy page and can manage ad personalization through Google Ad Settings.

During site-quality review or policy maintenance, advertising scripts may be limited to content pages and withheld from policy, contact, archive, or utility pages. This helps keep ads away from pages whose main purpose is navigation, disclosure, or site administration rather than reader content.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

Third-party services used on this site may place cookies or process technical request data needed to deliver measurement, fraud prevention, ad serving, or performance features. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Content for General Information Only

This site does not require account registration and does not offer public commenting. If you contact the site owner by email or through a public channel, the information you provide is used to understand and respond to that request.

Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

PetStorie does not ask readers to create accounts, submit pet medical records, upload private documents, provide payment details, leave public comments, or submit email addresses through newsletter forms. If you follow an external link, that third-party site may collect information under its own policies.

Questions

If you need to report a privacy concern, correction request, or policy question, please use the contact options listed on the contact page.
