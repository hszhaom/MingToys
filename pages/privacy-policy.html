Privacy notice

Privacy Policy

This site provides informational content. It does not currently run a third-party web analytics service or load third-party advertising scripts. Basic technical request data may still be processed by the hosting provider to operate, secure, and deliver the website.

Site Operator

PetStorie is an independent personal website. Privacy questions can be sent to zhaoming990@gmail.com.

Accounts, Comments, Email Lists, and Affiliate Links

PetStorie does not provide user accounts, login features, public comments, newsletter signup forms, paid memberships, or checkout features. The site does not currently use affiliate marketing links. If those features are added later, this policy will be updated before they are used.

Analytics

PetStorie does not currently use a third-party web analytics service. The site does not ask readers to create an account or identify themselves for normal browsing.

Advertising

PetStorie has prepared for a possible future Google AdSense integration, but it does not currently load Google advertising scripts or display Google ads. Before advertising is enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the services in use, applicable cookie choices, and links to Google's advertising information and settings.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

PetStorie does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies at this time. External websites linked from PetStorie may collect information under their own terms and privacy practices. Hosting providers may process technical request data needed for security, fraud prevention, and delivery of the site.

Content for General Information Only

This site does not require account registration and does not offer public commenting. If you contact the site owner by email or through a public channel, the information you provide is used to understand and respond to that request.

Data We Do Not Intentionally Collect

PetStorie does not ask readers to create accounts, submit pet medical records, upload private documents, provide payment details, leave public comments, or submit email addresses through newsletter forms. If you follow an external link, that third-party site may collect information under its own policies.

Questions

If you need to report a privacy concern, correction request, or policy question, please use the contact options listed on the contact page.
