My Background With Dogs

PetStorie is a personal publishing project founded and edited by ming.zhao in Hong Kong. The editor has more than 12 years of hands-on dog-care experience and writes for English-speaking readers who want practical breed research before choosing a dog.

That experience includes caring for Golden Retrievers for around five years, Boston Terriers for around three years, Toy Poodles for around two years, Shiba Inus for around six years, and Pembroke Welsh Corgis for around four years. Some care periods overlap; this is not a claim of twenty consecutive years of ownership.

First-hand observations are limited to the breeds the editor has actually cared for. They may inform practical questions about routine, grooming, exercise, cost planning, and owner fit, but they do not replace professional veterinary or training expertise and are never used as proof of a health or behavior claim across an entire breed.

PetStorie does not claim veterinary review, certified trainer review, or breed-club endorsement unless a specific page says so. When a topic depends on an individual dog's health, behavior, diet, or safety, readers should use the article as preparation for a conversation with a veterinarian, qualified trainer, breeder, rescue, or shelter professional.

What Shapes Each Guide

A PetStorie guide starts with the harder part of ownership, not the most flattering trait. A low-shedding coat may also mean a demanding grooming schedule. A family-friendly reputation may still require adult supervision, training, and a realistic match with the household's time. A large dog may be gentle but expensive to feed, insure, transport, and care for.

My role is to turn those trade-offs into useful research questions. Published pages are checked against the page-level sources shown on each guide, and first-hand notes are limited to the breeds listed above. This keeps personal experience in its proper place: practical context, not proof of a medical, behavioral, or breed-wide claim.

What You Can Expect From PetStorie

  • Breed guides that explain what daily life with a dog is likely to involve.
  • Topic collections such as apartment dogs, low-shedding breeds, family dogs, and large breeds.
  • Plain-English explanations of grooming load, barking, heat sensitivity, exercise needs, and cost pressure.
  • Decision tools that help readers compare owner fit, routine, grooming, and health planning before choosing a dog.

What PetStorie Does Not Do

PetStorie does not replace veterinary care, emergency advice, or individualized behavior work. Articles are informational and are written to help readers prepare for professional conversations, not to diagnose or treat a dog. When a dog shows signs of illness, pain, injury, breathing trouble, collapse, seizures, or another urgent change, contact a veterinarian promptly.

The site does not claim veterinary review, certified trainer review, breed-club endorsement, or paid expert authorship unless a specific page says so. Individual dogs vary by age, health history, training, environment, and early life experience; a breed guide can narrow research, but it cannot guarantee a fit for a particular home.

How to Use the Site

Start with a collection page if you are still comparing broad options, then read the full breed guide for the dogs on your shortlist. Use the dog cost calculator and fit score cards to pressure-test a decision, not to replace a conversation with a breeder, rescue, veterinarian, trainer, landlord, or insurer.
