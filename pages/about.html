About the site

About PetStorie

PetStorie is an independent content site focused on practical dog breed guides for everyday owners. We publish long-form articles that compare temperament, grooming, exercise, health considerations, training challenges, and owner fit.

The goal is simple: help readers choose a dog they can realistically care for, not just the one that looks appealing in a photo or trend post.

PetStorie Editorial Team

PetStorie is written and maintained by the PetStorie editorial team, an independent site team focused on practical dog breed research for English-speaking readers. The team edits guides for clarity, owner fit, conservative health language, internal linking, and source transparency.

We do not present the site as veterinary care, certified training advice, or breed-club endorsement. When a topic depends on an individual dog's health, behavior, diet, or safety, readers should use the article as preparation for a conversation with a veterinarian, qualified trainer, breeder, rescue, or shelter professional.

What We Publish

  • Breed guides that explain what daily life with a dog is likely to involve.
  • Topic collections such as apartment dogs, low-shedding breeds, family dogs, and large breeds.
  • Plain-English explanations of common trade-offs, including grooming load, barking, heat sensitivity, and exercise needs.
  • Decision tools that help readers compare owner fit, cost, exercise, grooming, and health planning before choosing a dog.

How We Approach Breed Advice

PetStorie is built around practical owner fit. A breed profile should not read like a sales page. It should help a reader understand what the dog is like on an ordinary weekday: how much movement it needs, how it handles people, what grooming really costs, which health risks are worth asking about, and what kind of owner will struggle.

We avoid promising that any breed is perfect for every home. Individual dogs vary by age, line, health history, training, and early life experience. The site is meant to narrow research and help readers ask better questions before contacting a rescue, shelter, breeder, veterinarian, or trainer.

What We Do Not Do

PetStorie does not replace veterinary care, emergency advice, or individualized behavior work. Articles are informational and are written to help with research, not diagnosis or treatment. When a dog shows signs of illness, pain, injury, breathing trouble, collapse, seizures, or other urgent symptoms, readers should contact a veterinarian promptly.

How to Use the Site

Start with a breed collection page if you are still comparing broad options. Use individual breed guides when you want a more detailed view of temperament, owner fit, health concerns, and care routines. Readers can also browse the full archive for all published guides.

Corrections and Transparency

PetStorie is maintained as an independent informational site. If a page contains a broken link, unclear wording, factual concern, or outdated recommendation, readers can report it through the public contact channel. We do not claim veterinary review, professional training certification, or breed-club endorsement unless that is explicitly stated on a page.

More detail is available in the editorial policy and data sources statement.
